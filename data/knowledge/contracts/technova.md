Rates are firm for the full term of this agreement. **No rate revision applies unless agreed in a written amendment signed by both parties** and notified at least 30 days before it takes effect. No amendment has been signed to date.

### 2.6 Tax and inclusions
Prices exclude GST, charged as CGST 9% + SGST 9% (supplier and buyer both in Karnataka). Delivery to Acme stores in Bengaluru is included.

## 3. Payment terms

### 3.1 Due date
Payment is due **30 days** from the date of a valid invoice, in Acme's next Tuesday or
Friday payment run after approval.

### 3.2 Bank account
Payments are made by NEFT/RTGS to: HDFC Bank, A/c ending 4417, IFSC HDFC0000123.

## 4. Penalties and service levels

### 4.1 Penalties
Late delivery: 0.5% of the order value per week of delay, capped at 5%. Defective units are replaced within 7 days at no cost.

## 5. Other terms

### 5.1 Warranty
All hardware carries a 1-year onsite replacement warranty from the date of delivery.

### 5.2 Invoicing
Invoices must quote the Acme PO number (where a PO applies) and comply with GST Rule 46.
