# Supply agreement — Sharma Office Supplies

Agreement ACME/ADM/2026/011 between **Acme Retail Pvt Ltd** (GSTIN 29AACCA4821K1ZM, Bengaluru) and
**Sharma Office Supplies** (GSTIN 27AKZPS6612M1Z4) for the supply of stationery and consumables.

## 1. Term and parties

### 1.1 Term
This agreement is valid from **2026-04-01** to **2027-03-31**.

### 1.2 Contract owner
Acme's contract owner is Anita Rao (Admin).

### 2.1 A4 copier paper 75gsm (ream of 500)
Agreed rate: **₹230 per REAM** (HSN/SAC 4802), valid 2026-04-01 to 2026-09-30.

### 2.2 A4 copier paper 75gsm (ream of 500) — revised
Agreed rate: **₹240 per REAM** (HSN/SAC 4802), valid 2026-10-01 to 2027-03-31 (Amendment 1).

### 2.3 Ball pen, blue (box of 50)
Agreed rate: **₹350 per BOX** (HSN/SAC 9608), valid 2026-04-01 to 2027-03-31.

### 2.4 Whiteboard marker set (4 colours)
Agreed rate: **₹180 per SET** (HSN/SAC 9608), valid 2026-04-01 to 2027-03-31.

### 2.5 Thermal receipt roll 79mm
Agreed rate: **₹42 per ROLL** (HSN/SAC 4811), valid 2026-04-01 to 2027-03-31.

### 2.6 Rate revisions
**Amendment 1 (signed 2026-09-15):** the copier paper rate is revised from ₹230 to ₹240 per ream with effect from 2026-10-01, owing to higher pulp prices. All other rates are unchanged.

### 2.7 Tax and inclusions
Supplies are dispatched from Mumbai, so IGST applies (copier paper 12%, other items 18%). Freight to Bengaluru is included in the rates and may not be charged separately.

## 3. Payment terms

### 3.1 Due date
Payment is due **15 days** from the date of a valid invoice, in Acme's next Tuesday or
Friday payment run after approval.

### 3.2 Bank account
Payments are made by NEFT/RTGS to: ICICI Bank, A/c ending 0921, IFSC ICIC0001045.

## 4. Penalties and service levels

### 4.1 Penalties
Short or damaged supplies are replaced within 5 working days. Repeated short supply (3 times in a quarter) allows Acme to terminate with 15 days' notice.

## 5. Other terms

### 5.1 Part deliveries
Orders may be delivered and invoiced in parts. Each invoice must quote the PO number, and the total invoiced may not exceed the PO total.

### 5.2 Invoicing
Invoices must quote the Acme PO number (where a PO applies) and comply with GST Rule 46.
