# Supply agreement — BlueLeaf Logistics Pvt Ltd

Agreement ACME/OPS/2026/007 between **Acme Retail Pvt Ltd** (GSTIN 29AACCA4821K1ZM, Bengaluru) and
**BlueLeaf Logistics Pvt Ltd** (GSTIN 33AAECB7720Q1ZE) for the road transport and last-mile delivery services.

## 1. Term and parties

### 1.1 Term
This agreement is valid from **2026-04-01** to **2027-03-31**.

### 1.2 Contract owner
Acme's contract owner is Vikram Iyer (Operations).

## 2. Rates

| Item / service | HSN/SAC | Unit | Agreed rate (excl. GST) | Validity |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Line haul Chennai–Bengaluru, 32ft container | 9965 | TRIP | ₹11,800 | 2026-04-01 to 2026-08-31 |
| Line haul Chennai–Bengaluru, 32ft container — revised | 9965 | TRIP | ₹12,500 | 2026-09-01 to 2027-03-31 (Amendment 1) |
| Last-mile delivery, Bengaluru stores | 9965 | DROP | ₹450 | 2026-04-01 to 2027-03-31 |

### 2.1 Line haul Chennai–Bengaluru, 32ft container
Agreed rate: **₹11,800 per TRIP** (HSN/SAC 9965), valid 2026-04-01 to 2026-08-31.

### 2.2 Line haul Chennai–Bengaluru, 32ft container — revised
Agreed rate: **₹12,500 per TRIP** (HSN/SAC 9965), valid 2026-09-01 to 2027-03-31 (Amendment 1).

### 2.3 Last-mile delivery, Bengaluru stores
Agreed rate: **₹450 per DROP** (HSN/SAC 9965), valid 2026-04-01 to 2027-03-31.

### 2.4 Rate revisions
**Amendment 1 (signed 2026-08-20):** fuel-price escalation — the line-haul rate is revised from ₹11,800 to ₹12,500 per trip with effect from 2026-09-01.

### 2.5 Tax and inclusions
GST on goods transport is charged at 12% IGST under forward charge (supplier in Tamil Nadu). Each trip needs a signed proof of delivery; Acme's receiving store raises a GRN on receipt, and **invoices are paid only against a GRN**.

## 3. Payment terms

### 3.1 Due date
Payment is due **30 days** from the date of a valid invoice, in Acme's next Tuesday or
Friday payment run after approval.

### 3.2 Bank account
Payments are made by NEFT/RTGS to: State Bank of India, A/c ending 3305, IFSC SBIN0004821.

## 4. Penalties and service levels

### 4.1 Penalties
Detention beyond 24 hours at Acme's premises is payable at ₹1,500 per day. Delivery more than 48 hours late attracts a deduction of 5% of the trip charge.

## 5. Other terms

### 5.1 Invoicing
Invoices must quote the Acme PO number (where a PO applies) and comply with GST Rule 46.
