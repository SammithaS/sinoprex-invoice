# Supply agreement — Kaveri Print House

Agreement ACME/MKT/2026/002 between **Acme Retail Pvt Ltd** (GSTIN 29AACCA4821K1ZM, Bengaluru) and
**Kaveri Print House** (GSTIN 29BNKPK3389D1ZN) for the printing of marketing collateral.

## 1. Term and parties

### 1.1 Term
This agreement is valid from **2026-04-01** to **2027-03-31**.

### 1.2 Contract owner
Acme's contract owner is Sneha Kulkarni (Marketing).

### 2.5 Tax and inclusions
Rates exclude GST at 18%. Kaveri Print House and Acme are both registered in Karnataka, so **CGST 9% + SGST 9%** applies; IGST must not be charged.

### 2.6 Proofs
Design proofs are approved by Acme Marketing before printing. Reprints caused by Kaveri's errors are free of charge.

## 3. Payment terms

### 3.1 Due date
Payment is due **15 days** from the date of a valid invoice, in Acme's next Tuesday or
Friday payment run after approval.

### 3.2 Bank account
Payments are made by NEFT/RTGS to: Canara Bank, A/c ending 7781, IFSC CNRB0002210.

## 4. Penalties and service levels

### 4.1 Penalties
Delivery later than the agreed campaign date: 10% deduction on the affected line.

## 5. Other terms

### 5.1 Part invoicing
Large print runs may be invoiced in parts as they are delivered, each referencing the PO.

### 5.2 Invoicing
Invoices must quote the Acme PO number (where a PO applies) and comply with GST Rule 46.
