# Supply agreement — Deccan Facility Services LLP

Agreement ACME/ADM/2026/005 between **Acme Retail Pvt Ltd** (GSTIN 29AACCA4821K1ZM, Bengaluru) and

This agreement is valid from **2026-04-01** to **2027-03-31**.

### 2.1 Housekeeping services, HQ (team of 14, 6 days a week)
Agreed rate: **₹1,85,000 per MONTH** (HSN/SAC 998533), valid 2026-04-01 to 2027-03-31.

Friday payment run after approval.

### 3.2 Bank account
Payments are made by NEFT/RTGS to: Axis Bank, A/c ending 5502, IFSC UTIB0000561.

Each monthly audit score below 85%: 2% deduction from that month's fee.
