# Supply agreement — Rangoli Packaging Industries

Agreement ACME/OPS/2026/009 between **Acme Retail Pvt Ltd** (GSTIN 29AACCA4821K1ZM, Bengaluru) and
**Rangoli Packaging Industries** (GSTIN 24AAPFR8813C1Z5) for the supply of packaging materials.

## 1. Term and parties

### 1.1 Term
This agreement is valid from **2026-04-01** to **2027-03-31**.

### 1.2 Contract owner
Acme's contract owner is Vikram Iyer (Operations).

## 2. Rates

| Item / service | HSN/SAC | Unit | Agreed rate (excl. GST) | Validity |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Corrugated box 5-ply 18x12x12 in | 4819 | NOS | ₹36 | 2026-04-01 to 2026-09-30 |
| Corrugated box 5-ply 18x12x12 in — revised | 4819 | NOS | ₹38 | 2026-10-01 to 2027-03-31 (Amendment 1) |
| Packing tape 48mm x 65m | 3919 | ROLL | ₹62 | 2026-04-01 to 2027-03-31 |

### 2.1 Corrugated box 5-ply 18x12x12 in
Agreed rate: **₹36 per NOS** (HSN/SAC 4819), valid 2026-04-01 to 2026-09-30.

### 2.2 Corrugated box 5-ply 18x12x12 in — revised
Agreed rate: **₹38 per NOS** (HSN/SAC 4819), valid 2026-10-01 to 2027-03-31 (Amendment 1).

### 2.3 Packing tape 48mm x 65m
Agreed rate: **₹62 per ROLL** (HSN/SAC 3919), valid 2026-04-01 to 2027-03-31.

### 2.4 Rate revisions
**Amendment 1 (signed 2026-09-10):** kraft-paper escalation — the 5-ply box rate is revised from ₹36 to ₹38 per box with effect from 2026-10-01.

### 2.5 Tax and inclusions
GST at 18% IGST (supplier in Gujarat). Freight to Bengaluru is included.

## 3. Payment terms

### 3.1 Due date
Payment is due **45 days** from the date of a valid invoice, in Acme's next Tuesday or
Friday payment run after approval.

### 3.2 Bank account
Payments are made by NEFT/RTGS to: Bank of Baroda, Vadodara, A/c ending 6634, IFSC BARB0VADODA.

## 4. Penalties and service levels

### 4.1 Penalties
Boxes failing the burst-strength test are replaced free of charge; a second failure in a quarter attracts a 5% deduction on that delivery.

## 5. Other terms

### 5.1 Bank details
Payments are made only to the bank account named in section 3.2. **Any change of bank account must be notified in writing on Rangoli's letterhead, signed by an authorised signatory, and Acme will confirm it by calling the contact on file before paying into the new account.** A new account printed on an invoice is not a valid notification.

### 5.2 Invoicing
Invoices must quote the Acme PO number (where a PO applies) and comply with GST Rule 46.
