# Supply agreement — CloudNest Software Pvt Ltd

Agreement ACME/IT/2026/001 between **Acme Retail Pvt Ltd** (GSTIN 29AACCA4821K1ZM, Bengaluru) and
**CloudNest Software Pvt Ltd** (GSTIN 29AAICC9054H1ZA) for the subscription to the CloudNest Retail POS Suite (SaaS).

## 1. Term and parties

### 1.1 Term
This agreement is valid from **2026-04-01** to **2027-03-31**.

### 1.2 Contract owner
Acme's contract owner is Rahul Menon (IT).

## 2. Rates

| Item / service | HSN/SAC | Unit | Agreed rate (excl. GST) | Validity |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| CloudNest Retail POS Suite — 60 user licences, monthly subscription | 997331 | MONTH | ₹42,000 | 2026-04-01 to 2027-03-31 |

### 2.1 CloudNest Retail POS Suite — 60 user licences, monthly subscription
Agreed rate: **₹42,000 per MONTH** (HSN/SAC 997331), valid 2026-04-01 to 2027-03-31.

### 2.2 Billing
Billed monthly in advance on the 1st of each month. Additional licences cost ₹700 per user per month, pro-rated.

### 2.3 No purchase order
This is a recurring subscription: **no purchase order is raised**. Under the AP policy, non-PO invoices need approval by the Department Head — IT. These invoices have been coded to **GL 6420 (Software subscriptions)** and **cost centre CC-IT-01**.

### 2.4 Tax
GST at 18% (CGST 9% + SGST 9%; both parties in Karnataka).

## 3. Payment terms

### 3.1 Due date
Payment is due **7 days** from the date of a valid invoice, in Acme's next Tuesday or
Friday payment run after approval.

### 3.2 Bank account
Payments are made by NEFT/RTGS to: Kotak Mahindra Bank, A/c ending 1196, IFSC KKBK0008011.

## 4. Penalties and service levels

### 4.1 Penalties
Service availability below 99.5% in a month earns a service credit of 5% of that month's fee for each 0.5% shortfall, capped at 25%.

## 5. Other terms

### 5.1 Invoicing
Invoices must quote the Acme PO number (where a PO applies) and comply with GST Rule 46.
